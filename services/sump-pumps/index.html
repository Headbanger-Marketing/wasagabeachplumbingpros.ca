Questions About the Work

Can the sump pit be reached without disturbing stored items?

Boxes, shelving or flooring beside a pit may need moving, and a dated photograph of the area as it stood helps explain any later mark or dampness on stored items.

  • Photograph the pit surround and the way to it

    Whether the pit, check valve and backwater valve are reached from the same area is the first thing for the respondent to confirm. Photograph the floor around each, the discharge pipe where it leaves the wall and the route from the stairs, with the date visible, and list the stored items you will clear. Do not lift a cover or cap yourself.

  • Compare working around the pit with clearing it

    Compare a plan that works around shelving and stored items with one that needs the whole space cleared, and ask the respondent which is expected in your Wasaga Beach home. Photograph the arrangement first so items can be returned to the same places.

  • Get a written record of the pit area after work

    Ask the provider to describe in writing what was done at the pit, valve and discharge line, and how the floor and stored items were left. Photograph the same area again and keep the new photographs with your first set. Have anything noticed outside the agreed work, such as a worn cover or a damp corner, recorded as a separate item.
